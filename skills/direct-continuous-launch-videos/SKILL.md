# Direct Continuous Launch Videos

1. Define the product, audience, real feature to prove, exact CTA, duration and target ratio. Use the user's brand kit, approved copy and recordings. Make a timed beat sheet with subject, camera motion, screen action, copy, sound cue and intended end frame.
2. Apply OneTake's continuous-camera idea when requested. Carry a visible anchor into the next beat and align position, scale and movement direction at each boundary. Inspect the frame before, on and after every join. Keep a deliberate cut when it makes an action or change of context easier to read.
3. Use Cinetic's filmed-product approach for a short reveal. Build a single proof moment around the real screen action, then hold the CTA long enough to read. Start with a preview of transitions and exact copy before committing to costly motion blur or a long final render.
4. Apply huashu-motion's frame-specific review workflow. Accept feedback with a timestamp or frame number and state which source scene changed. Spot one sound cue per meaningful action. Keep voice, music and effects as separate stems when available, and verify music rights and intelligibility.
5. Render with the project's chosen engine. Check frame continuity, UI legibility at phone size, the audio mix and end-card hold. Deliver the editable composition and review cut. In PipelineX, treat the launch film as its own project or a reviewed insert.
