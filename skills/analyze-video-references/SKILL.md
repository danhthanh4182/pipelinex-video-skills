# Analyze Video References

1. Obtain the video or visible frames. Record its provenance, duration, aspect ratio and sample times. Describe only observed details, and label guesses about the renderer or missing footage as uncertain.
2. For Rea-Video, default to the user's fork at `danhthanh4182/rea-video` when working on their clips. Verify the checkout and current CLI commands before running the workflow. Sample local media with FFprobe and FFmpeg first. Use external vision only when the user asks for that route and has cleared the media for transfer. Record each shot's time range, purpose, framing, subject, motion, transition, typography and sound. Use the published VIDEO.md specification if the handoff requires compatibility. For unavailable media, separate what the brief says from what you observed.
3. Use Lemo-Opuscar to study palettes, lens and camera cues, pacing and typography. Use Awesome Opus 5.5 Videos as a collection of references. Map those traits to the actual brand and audience. Write an original storyboard and shot direction with unique artwork, copy, and layouts.
4. For PipelineX, map the storyboard to editorial beats in the approved script. Add the new shot's timing, planned visual, renderer, captions and source or asset credit. Keep editorial claims tied to the dossier. Hand back a storyboard and one representative keyframe or frame description.
